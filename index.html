How it works

  1. Copy a Google Sheet. One link, one click.
  2. Answer a few questions in the panel that opens in the sheet. About 5 minutes.
  3. Share your link or show the QR code at the gym.

Everything people enter lands in your Google account, not on some server. This page only shows it.
